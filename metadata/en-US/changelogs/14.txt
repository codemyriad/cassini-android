Cassini talk 0.4 is a new app: it installs beside Cassini 0.3 and does not update it. Save the notes you want to keep as .opus documents in the old app and open them here.

* In English, Italian, French, Spanish and Portuguese.
* Import any audio file, or share one from another app.
* Share a note, its transcript or its subtitles.
* Playback speed, and controls on the lock screen.
* A smaller model download, about 530 MB.
